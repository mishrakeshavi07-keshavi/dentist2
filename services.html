Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept walk-in appointments?+

We recommend booking ahead via call, WhatsApp, or our contact form, but we do our best to accommodate genuine walk-in emergencies during clinic hours.

Is scaling and cleaning painful?+

No — routine scaling is generally painless. Sensitive patients can request a numbing gel beforehand for extra comfort.

Do you offer payment plans for implants or braces?+

Yes, we offer transparent instalment plans for treatments above a certain value — ask our front desk for current options.

How early should children have their first dental visit?+

We recommend a first visit around age three, or earlier if you notice any discoloration, discomfort, or feeding difficulty.
