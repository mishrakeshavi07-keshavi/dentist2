Our story

From one dental chair to a full studio of care

Dr. Ananya Rathore opens a small practice in Chandpole with one chair and a promise: honest advice, always.

2007

Move to C-Scheme

Growing patient trust leads to a bigger studio with dedicated rooms for surgery, X-ray, and recovery.

Smilecraft becomes one of the first Jaipur clinics to adopt digital scanning and laser treatments.

2026

25 years, 18,000+ smiles

Today, a five-dentist team continues the same founding promise for a new generation of patients.

Meet the founder

Dr. Ananya Rathore

BDS, MDS (Prosthodontics) · Chief Dental Surgeon · 25 years in practice

Dr. Rathore founded Smilecraft in 1999 after completing her postgraduate specialisation in Prosthodontics. Over 25 years, she has led the clinic's shift toward painless, technology-assisted dentistry, while keeping the same unhurried, explain-everything approach she started with.

She is supported by a resident team of four specialists covering orthodontics, pediatric dentistry, oral surgery, and cosmetic dentistry — so every patient sees the right expert for their treatment.
